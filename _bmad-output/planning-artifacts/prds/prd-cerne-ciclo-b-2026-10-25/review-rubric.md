# PRD Quality Review — Cerne

## Overall verdict

This is an unusually honest, well-disciplined PRD for its stakes: its Open Questions, `[ASSUMPTION]` tags, and Non-Goals do real work instead of performing completeness, its 43 FRs / 6 UJs / IDs are contiguous and cross-reference cleanly, and the Cross-Cutting NFRs and Failure-contract table are specific enough to be checked, not adjectives. The two real soft spots are **done-ness** — a handful of FRs (FR-11, FR-15, FR-20) and one NFR (RNF01) have no settled acceptance path despite `status: final` — and **strategic coherence** — RF08 (Proactive Insights) is the one feature with no stated User-Journey anchor, the single break in an otherwise-complete thesis→UJ→feature chain. Neither defect is fatal or expensive to close, but both are exactly the kind of thing a solo author should resolve deliberately before build reaches those units, not discover mid-implementation.

## Decision-readiness — strong

Decisions are stated as decisions (§1's "bet is narrow and deliberate: not a bigger feature set than competitors, but a better memory"), trade-offs name what was given up (FR-20: "no tag/category filter exists, since categorization is an extension feature"), and §8's eleven Open Questions mostly read as genuinely unresolved rather than rhetorical — several are wired directly into the FR they block (`[OPEN]` tags inline at FR-11, FR-15, FR-20), which is exactly the behavior the rubric wants instead of a quiet gap. SM-5 is explicitly marked "informal" and "not a graded criterion" rather than dressed up as a real metric. The one `[NOTE FOR PM]` (§4.11, under FR-43) sits at a real tension — a missing account-deletion confirmation screen — not a safe checkpoint.

### Findings

- **medium** Open Questions aren't sequenced against the delivery order they constrain (§8, §6.1) — OQ-3 blocks FR-11 inside RF01 (build unit 2 of 11) and OQ-7 blocks FR-19/20 inside RF06 (unit 6), but §8 lists all eleven OQs as a flat, unprioritized numbered list with no marker for "must resolve before unit N starts." A solo author sequencing their own work benefits from knowing which OQs are load-bearing soon vs. deferrable to polish time. *Fix:* tag each OQ with the earliest build unit it blocks, or sort §8 by urgency against §6.1's order.
- **low** OQ-5 ("Cloud-only, immutable-entry design is a backend constraint... confirm there's no appetite to request a contract change") is not actually open — the same sentence answers it ("there is none per `AGENTS.md`"). The PRD is self-aware about this ("recorded here only so it isn't silently re-discovered as a bug later"), so the risk is cosmetic, but it inflates the "genuinely open" count a reader would use to gauge readiness. *Fix:* move OQ-5 to a "Decisions already settled, recorded for visibility" note rather than the Open Questions list.

## Substance over theater — strong

No findings. The persona is a single, consistently-named protagonist (Mara) reused across all six UJs rather than a persona roster assembled for appearance. The Cross-Cutting NFRs are quantified (RNF01: "<100ms" / "300ms"; RNF07: "≥80%"; RNF08: "zero hardcoded visual values") rather than boilerplate ("must be scalable/secure"). The Vision (§1) is specific to this product's bet and the addendum's competitive section names real, specific competitor complaints (Mindsera "feature-sprawl," Rosebud's "therapist-designed framing risk") rather than generic differentiation language — this reads as earned, not templated.

## Strategic coherence — adequate

The thesis ("a better memory," §1) is stated plainly and every feature section but one explicitly declares which UJ it realizes, which is the right mechanism for proving a PRD isn't a backlog with headers. Success Metrics are honestly scoped to conformance rather than adoption, and the PRD says so itself (§7: "this PRD's graded metrics (SM-1–3) can only confirm the RAG-memory mechanism is wired correctly end-to-end... is not a graded surface for this delivery") — for an academic-stakes deliverable this is the right call, explicitly owned rather than smoothed over.

### Findings

- **medium** §4.9 Proactive Insights (RF08 / FR-31–33) is the only feature section that does not declare a "Realizes UJ-X" linkage — every other section in §4 does (4.1 through 4.8, 4.10, 4.11 all state one). RF08's description reads "Depends on RF04" and stops. This breaks the otherwise-complete thesis→UJ→feature traceability chain the rest of the document relies on, and leaves FR-31–33 structurally un-anchored to any user journey. *Fix:* either add a UJ realized by RF08 (e.g., extend UJ-2 to cover an unprompted Insight arriving mid-conversation, which the current UJ-2 edge case does not mention), or state explicitly that RF08 is UJ-less by design and why.

## Done-ness clarity — adequate

Most FRs carry a single self-evident testable clause or an explicit Consequences block, and several are impressively concrete (FR-38's "correctly at zero, one, and more-than-thirty Entries"; FR-21's "debounced... by 300ms"; FR-31's "5 user messages sent or 2 minutes of inactivity, whichever occurs first"). None of the classic vague tells ("gracefully," "reasonable," "user-friendly") appear in an FR statement. But a non-trivial slice of the 43 FRs, plus one NFR, do not actually have a settled "done" at this status.

### Findings

- **high** RNF01 (performance: "<100ms" local UI, "300ms" loading-indicator threshold) is the only one of the nine Cross-Cutting NFRs with no mapped Success Metric or stated verification method. SM-2 explicitly ties to RNF06+RNF07, SM-3 to RNF09, SM-4 to RNF08 — but no SM references RNF01, and §7 gives no instrumented or manual method (profiling pass? instrumented test? stopwatch?) for checking it. For a document whose whole Success Metrics framing is "success is defined by specification conformance" (§7), a conformance criterion with no check is a gap. *Fix:* either add an SM (or a line under SM-1) that names how RNF01 gets verified, or state explicitly that RNF01 is unverified-by-design for this delivery.
- **medium** Three FRs have a literal unresolved sub-behavior at `status: final`: FR-11 ("`[OPEN]` Switching tabs mid-recording... is undefined behavior in the current prototype"), FR-15 ("`[OPEN]` No retry affordance is visible on the error state"), and FR-20 ("`[OPEN]` How an active text search and active filters interact... is unspecified"). These are honestly flagged (good — this is exactly what explicit scope-honesty should look like), but it means an engineer building RF01 (unit 2 of 11) or RF06 (unit 6) cannot fully determine "done" for that sub-behavior without first resolving OQ-3/OQ-6/OQ-7. *Fix:* no PRD change needed beyond what §8 already does — but see the Decision-readiness finding above about sequencing these against build order, since FR-11's gap is live almost immediately.
- **medium** FR-19 (Semantic search) states the behavior with an example ("'ansiedade' matches... 'nervosismo'") but, unlike RF05/RF08/RF09 which each explicitly declare generated-content *quality* out of acceptance scope (§5: "Grading or verifying the quality of AI-generated text... RF05/RF08/RF09 are scoped to mechanism... quality is explicitly out of acceptance scope"), RF06 search-result quality has no such explicit carve-out. §8 OQ-10 raises this exact "mechanism-only" boundary question for RF04 only — RF06 has the identical ambiguity (is "returns semantically correct results" part of what's graded, or only "calls the endpoint and renders whatever comes back"?) and it isn't named anywhere. *Fix:* extend OQ-10 (or add an OQ-12) to cover RF06 search-quality alongside RF04 chat-quality.
- **low** RNF02 ("PII stripping runs via a Claude Haiku 4.5... step, server-side only") and RNF03 ("Zero data retention by upstream AI providers") describe backend/vendor facts the Flutter app neither implements nor can verify — they're inherited context, not app-testable requirements, yet they sit in the same "Cross-Cutting NFRs" list as RNF01/04/05-09 which *are* app behaviors. This blurs what this PRD's own deliverable is actually on the hook for. *Fix:* either mark RNF02/RNF03 as "inherited, not verified by this delivery" inline, or move them to the addendum's backend-context section alongside the "tech stack already fixed upstream" note (which already does this for the AI models themselves).

## Scope honesty — strong

The Non-Goals section (§5) does real work — eight explicit exclusions, three tagged `[ext]` and tied to concrete inert-placeholder descriptions (e.g., persona adjustment "exists only as a cosmetic Settings selector... that does not change assistant behavior in this delivery"), plus the risk-signal-detection exclusion is framed as "a deliberate scope exclusion... not an oversight." The two `[ASSUMPTION]` tags (§2.1/§2.3 persona reconstruction, §4.1 FR-1 signup fields) both round-trip cleanly into §9's Assumptions Index with no orphaned or missing entries. De-scoping is proposed openly rather than silently assumed (§5's AI-categorization and goal-extraction items both state what they're downgraded to, not just that they're cut).

No additional findings beyond what's already captured under Decision-readiness (OQ-5 mislabeling) and Done-ness clarity (the FR-11/15/20 `[OPEN]` tags) — both are scope-honesty mechanisms working as intended, not failures of this dimension.

## Downstream usability — strong

Glossary (§3) terms are used consistently as proper nouns through the FRs (Entry, Chat Session, Chat Message, Sync Badge, Export Job). FR IDs run FR-1 through FR-43 with no gaps or duplicates (verified by section-by-section count: 3+4+4+2+5+4+5+3+3+4+6 = 43, matching §0's stated range); UJ-1 through UJ-6 and SM-1–5 plus SM-C1/C2 are likewise contiguous. Cross-references resolve on spot-check (FR-15→FR-17, the error-taxonomy table's FR-37/FR-13 references, UJ-2's FR-2/FR-26 citation) and the Failure-contract retryable-code list is consistent across three locations (the PRD's own table, §7 SM-3's "5×3=15" framing, and the addendum's dio-retry list) — all six retryable codes match exactly across all three. Each feature section (§4.1–§4.11) is reasonably self-contained, stating its own dependencies and UJ linkage inline rather than relying on "see above."

## Shape fit — strong

This is a consumer product with meaningful UX, and UJs with a named, consistent protagonist (Mara) are correctly load-bearing — six UJs, one per major feature cluster, neither over- nor under-formalized. The heavy formal apparatus (43 FRs, a 13-code failure taxonomy, explicit NFR thresholds) matches the stated "academic/TCC rigorous" stakes and the stated audience (§0: "the solo author... and the academic reviewers who evaluate it") rather than being over-built for a solo hobby project — the rigor is calibrated to who grades it. Greenfield framing is handled correctly (no stale brownfield references; §0 and AGENTS.md agree no Flutter project exists yet). As a chain-top document (feeds UX/architecture/story work, §0), the downstream-usability strength above is load-bearing and earned.

## Mechanical notes

- **Casing drift:** "Entry" is capitalized consistently as the Glossary-defined term through most of §4, but §5's Non-Goals heading text drifts to lowercase ("Dynamic AI categorization of **entries**," "Offline **entry** creation"). Similarly "Dashboard" is capitalized in §3's Glossary but appears lowercase through §4.11 and UJ-6 ("checks her settings **dashboard**"). Low-impact, but worth a pass before downstream extraction treats case as meaningful.
- **Unnumbered sections:** §0–§9 are numbered, but "Information Architecture" (between §3 and §4) and the three trailing sections (Cross-Cutting NFRs, Constraints and Guardrails, Aesthetic and Tone) carry no section numbers. Internal references to the latter use the section name as a pseudo-ID (e.g., FR-18's "see §Cross-Cutting NFRs" convention), which works but is inconsistent with the rest of the document's numeric cross-referencing.
- **Feature-specific NFR without an ID:** §4.5's BLoC-composition rule ("No BLoC in this feature imports another BLoC directly...") is referenced by SM-1 but, unlike the nine global RNF01–09, has no stable identifier of its own. If more feature-specific NFRs accumulate, this will need one to stay unambiguous under downstream extraction.
- **Assumptions Index and ID continuity otherwise check out clean:** both `[ASSUMPTION]` tags round-trip into §9 with no drift; FR/UJ/SM numbering has no gaps, duplicates, or unresolved forward references found on spot-check.
