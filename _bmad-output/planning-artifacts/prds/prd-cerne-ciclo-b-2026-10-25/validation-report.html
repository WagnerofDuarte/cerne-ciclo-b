Fair

Unusually honest, well-disciplined PRD for its academic/TCC-rigorous stakes — Open Questions, [ASSUMPTION] tags, and Non-Goals do real work rather than performing completeness, and IDs/glossary/cross-refs are clean for downstream use. The two soft spots the rubric found are done-ness (a few FRs and one NFR have no settled acceptance path despite status: final) and strategic coherence (one feature with no User-Journey anchor, breaking an otherwise-complete thesis→UJ→feature chain).

A second, independent pass checked the PRD against the frozen docs/input/ sources specifically for drift introduced by the two polish passes that ran after the original reconciliation. Fidelity is strong overall — delivery order, extension non-goals, error taxonomy, and the AI-provider/on-device-STT constraints all match exactly — but it surfaced one high-severity dropped prototype behavior (an OCR-failure recovery path the prototype documents that the PRD's FR-13 omits) plus a handful of smaller drift items. Combined with the rubric's RNF01 finding, the report carries two high-severity findings, which is what pulls the grade to Fair rather than Good — neither is expensive to close.

Decision-readiness

strong

Decisions are stated as decisions (§1's "bet is narrow and deliberate: not a bigger feature set than competitors, but a better memory"), trade-offs name what was given up (FR-20: "no tag/category filter exists, since categorization is an extension feature"), and §8's eleven Open Questions mostly read as genuinely unresolved rather than rhetorical — several are wired directly into the FR they block ([OPEN] tags inline at FR-11, FR-15, FR-20). SM-5 is explicitly marked "informal" and "not a graded criterion" rather than dressed up as a real metric. The one [NOTE FOR PM] (§4.11, under FR-43) sits at a real tension — a missing account-deletion confirmation screen — not a safe checkpoint.

medium

Open Questions aren't sequenced against the delivery order they constrain

§8, §6.1
OQ-3 blocks FR-11 inside RF01 (build unit 2 of 11) and OQ-7 blocks FR-19/20 inside RF06 (unit 6), but §8 lists all eleven OQs as a flat, unprioritized list with no marker for "must resolve before unit N starts." A solo author sequencing their own work benefits from knowing which OQs are load-bearing soon vs. deferrable to polish time.
Fix: Tag each OQ with the earliest build unit it blocks, or sort §8 by urgency against §6.1's order.
low

OQ-5 is not actually open

§8
"Cloud-only, immutable-entry design is a backend constraint... confirm there's no appetite to request a contract change" is answered in the same sentence ("there is none per AGENTS.md"). The PRD is self-aware about this, so the risk is cosmetic, but it inflates the "genuinely open" count a reader would use to gauge readiness.
Fix: Move OQ-5 to a "decisions already settled, recorded for visibility" note rather than the Open Questions list.

Substance over theater

strong

No findings. The persona is a single, consistently-named protagonist (Mara) reused across all six UJs rather than a persona roster assembled for appearance. Cross-Cutting NFRs are quantified (RNF01: "<100ms" / "300ms"; RNF07: "≥80%"; RNF08: "zero hardcoded visual values") rather than boilerplate. The Vision (§1) is specific to this product's bet and the addendum's competitive section names real, specific competitor complaints (Mindsera "feature-sprawl," Rosebud's "therapist-designed framing risk") rather than generic differentiation language.

Strategic coherence

adequate

The thesis ("a better memory," §1) is stated plainly and every feature section but one explicitly declares which UJ it realizes — the right mechanism for proving a PRD isn't a backlog with headers. Success Metrics are honestly scoped to conformance rather than adoption, and the PRD says so itself (§7).

medium

RF08 Proactive Insights has no "Realizes UJ-X" linkage

§4.9 / FR-31–33
Every other §4 section (4.1–4.8, 4.10, 4.11) states one; RF08's description reads "Depends on RF04" and stops. This breaks the otherwise-complete thesis→UJ→feature traceability chain and leaves FR-31–33 structurally un-anchored to any user journey.
Fix: Either add a UJ realized by RF08 (e.g., extend UJ-2 to cover an unprompted Insight arriving mid-conversation), or state explicitly that RF08 is UJ-less by design and why.

Done-ness clarity

adequate

Most FRs carry a self-evident testable clause or an explicit Consequences block, and several are impressively concrete (FR-38's "correctly at zero, one, and more-than-thirty Entries"; FR-21's "debounced... by 300ms"; FR-31's "5 user messages sent or 2 minutes of inactivity, whichever occurs first"). None of the classic vague tells ("gracefully," "reasonable," "user-friendly") appear in an FR statement. But a non-trivial slice of the 43 FRs, plus one NFR, do not actually have a settled "done" at this status.

high

RNF01 has no mapped Success Metric or verification method

Cross-Cutting NFRs / §7
RNF01 ("<100ms" local UI, "300ms" loading-indicator threshold) is the only one of the nine Cross-Cutting NFRs with no mapped SM. SM-2 ties to RNF06+RNF07, SM-3 to RNF09, SM-4 to RNF08 — no SM references RNF01, and §7 gives no instrumented or manual method for checking it. For a document whose Success Metrics framing is "success is defined by specification conformance," a conformance criterion with no check is a gap.
Fix: Add an SM (or a line under SM-1) naming how RNF01 gets verified, or state explicitly that RNF01 is unverified-by-design for this delivery.
medium

Three FRs have a literal unresolved sub-behavior at status: final

FR-11, FR-15, FR-20
FR-11 ("switching tabs mid-recording... is undefined behavior in the current prototype"), FR-15 ("no retry affordance is visible on the error state"), FR-20 ("how an active text search and active filters interact... is unspecified"). Honestly flagged — but an engineer building RF01 (unit 2 of 11) or RF06 (unit 6) cannot fully determine "done" for that sub-behavior without first resolving OQ-3/OQ-6/OQ-7.
Fix: No PRD change needed beyond what §8 already does — but sequence these against build order (see the Decision-readiness finding above); FR-11's gap is live almost immediately.
medium

FR-19 search quality has no mechanism-only carve-out

FR-19 / §5 / OQ-10
Unlike RF05/RF08/RF09, which each explicitly declare generated-content quality out of acceptance scope, RF06 search-result quality has no such carve-out. OQ-10 raises this exact "mechanism-only" boundary question for RF04 only — RF06 has the identical ambiguity and it isn't named anywhere.
Fix: Extend OQ-10 (or add an OQ-12) to cover RF06 search-quality alongside RF04 chat-quality.
low

RNF02/RNF03 are inherited backend/vendor facts, not app-testable requirements

Cross-Cutting NFRs
RNF02 (PII stripping, server-side) and RNF03 (zero data retention by upstream AI providers) describe facts the Flutter app neither implements nor can verify, yet sit in the same list as RNF01/04/05-09 which are app behaviors. This blurs what this PRD's own deliverable is actually on the hook for.
Fix: Mark RNF02/RNF03 as "inherited, not verified by this delivery" inline, or move them to the addendum's backend-context section.

strong

No additional findings beyond what's already captured under Decision-readiness (OQ-5 mislabeling) and Done-ness clarity (the FR-11/15/20 [OPEN] tags) — both are scope-honesty mechanisms working as intended, not failures of this dimension.

strong

Shape fit

The heavy formal apparatus (43 FRs, a 13-code failure taxonomy, explicit NFR thresholds) matches the stated "academic/TCC rigorous" stakes and audience. Greenfield framing is handled correctly. As a chain-top document, the downstream-usability strength above is load-bearing and earned.

high

Dropped "Salvar só a foto" OCR-failure fallback

§4.4 FR-13 vs. prototype-extract.md §2.3/§4
prototype-extract.md explicitly documents a second recovery path on OCR failure: a "Salvar só a foto" action that saves the Entry without extracted text, distinct from "Tentar novamente." FR-13's consequences only mention "error with retry, photo never discarded" — the save-without-text exit is absent, so acceptance testing would miss a documented state.
Fix: Add a consequence to FR-13: "On OCR failure, in addition to retry, the user can save the Entry with no extracted text ('Salvar só a foto')."
medium

Unsupported reinstall caveat on biometric preference

§4.2 FR-7
FR-7 states the biometric toggle "survives app restarts (but not reinstalls)," but requirements.md §6.1 and the FR itself say the preference is stored server-side via PATCH /account/settings; a server-side value is retrieved on re-login after reinstall, so the "not reinstalls" claim is unsupported by (and in tension with) the cited mechanism.
Fix: Remove the reinstall caveat, or replace with an [ASSUMPTION] tag if a specific client-side caching behavior is intended.
medium

New Chat Session creation's offline restriction dropped

§4.7 FR-25/FR-27
requirements.md §6.3 states POST /chat/sessions is "blocked offline" (distinct from GET /chat/sessions and message history, which work offline from cache). FR-27 ("Offline composer lock") only covers disabling the composer in an existing conversation; FR-25 ("New-conversation action") has no offline consequence, so the PRD doesn't carry forward that starting a new session is also blocked while offline.
Fix: Add a consequence to FR-25 or FR-27 stating that POST /chat/sessions is blocked offline, consistent with the existing-conversation composer lock.
low

Overstated citation on session-expiry overlay coverage

§4.1 FR-2
FR-2 says the overlay can appear on the timeline (RF07) or chat (RF04), "the two surfaces explicitly called out in requirements.md as session-dependent." Only RF07 explicitly states this in requirements.md text; the chat case is sourced from prototype-extract.md, not requirements.md's RF04 text.
Fix: Reword to cite both sources correctly: "...the timeline (RF07, called out explicitly in requirements.md) and chat (shown as the 'Sessão expirada' state in prototype-extract.md)."
low

Prototype/backend conflict on profile editing not flagged

§4.11 FR-41
prototype-extract.md §2.6 describes the avatar area as having "editable name/e-mail" inline. openapi.yaml's PATCH /account/settings explicitly rejects email changes. FR-41 correctly scopes itself to display-name editing only, but — unlike other prototype/backend conflicts the addendum already documents — this one is never called out, so a future reader could reintroduce an email-edit field by trusting the prototype literally.
Fix: Add a line to addendum.md's "Corrections carried forward" noting that the prototype's visual "editable e-mail" affordance is not buildable, and FR-41 deliberately omits it.

Mechanical notes

  • Casing drift: "Entry" is capitalized consistently as the Glossary-defined term through most of §4, but §5's Non-Goals heading text drifts to lowercase ("Dynamic AI categorization of entries," "Offline entry creation"). Similarly "Dashboard" is capitalized in §3's Glossary but appears lowercase through §4.11 and UJ-6 ("checks her settings dashboard").
  • Unnumbered sections: §0–§9 are numbered, but "Information Architecture" (between §3 and §4) and the three trailing sections (Cross-Cutting NFRs, Constraints and Guardrails, Aesthetic and Tone) carry no section numbers. Internal references use the section name as a pseudo-ID, which works but is inconsistent with the rest of the document's numeric cross-referencing.
  • Feature-specific NFR without an ID: §4.5's BLoC-composition rule is referenced by SM-1 but, unlike the nine global RNF01–09, has no stable identifier of its own. If more feature-specific NFRs accumulate, this will need one to stay unambiguous under downstream extraction.
  • Assumptions Index and ID continuity otherwise check out clean: both [ASSUMPTION] tags round-trip into §9 with no drift; FR/UJ/SM numbering has no gaps, duplicates, or unresolved forward references found on spot-check.
